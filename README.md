# 3D Torus Clock

**Live Application:** [https://nimabeh.github.io/time-torus/](https://nimabeh.github.io/time-torus/)

Time on the clock is not flat. This project reimagines time as a single, unbroken 1D space curve winding through a 3D helical torus ($S^1 \times S^1$). The entire 24-hour cycle is mapped into 24 continuous macro-loops, ensuring every unique moment occupies an exact, non-overlapping coordinate in space.

### Spatial Perspectives & Recursive Geometry
* **Front View:** Displays the macro-rhythm of the 24-hour day across the main circular path.
* **Side View:** Rotates the perspective to reveal the depth of the poloidal loops, showing minutes as smooth spirals around the tube.
* **Recursive Geometry:** Minutes and seconds follow the exact same toroidal logic. Seconds form micro-helices nested seamlessly within the minute loops, eliminating discrete "ticks" in favor of continuous flow.

### Dynamic Solar Synchronization
* **IP-Based Solar Tracking:** Detects your location via IP to calculate local sunrise and sunset times in real time.
* **Visual Boundaries:** Solar transitions are marked directly on the 3D wire spring, dividing the curve into local day and night segments.
* **Tone Shift:** The environment palette continuously transitions between soft daylight tones and deep Aurora night skies as the time-node travels across the solar threshold, in between day and night.
